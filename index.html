Coverse Cafe is a contemporary café in Bejai, Mangaluru, offering a relaxed space for coffee, food, desserts, and conversations. Located at Vertex Ashoka on Airport Road, the café provides a comfortable setting for casual meetups, work sessions, and everyday dining.

With a menu that brings together café favourites and continental dishes, Coverse Cafe focuses on creating an inviting experience where guests can enjoy good food in a modern, welcoming atmosphere.
